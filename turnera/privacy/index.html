Turnera Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Turnera is designed to respect your privacy. The developer of Turnera does not collect your data. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account.

Information we collect

Turnera does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information to the developer.

Specifically, Turnera does not:

  • Require user accounts or registration
  • Collect names or email addresses
  • Collect location data
  • Display advertising
  • Track your activity
  • Use analytics services
  • Share information with third parties

Your data and iCloud

Turnera stores the data you create on your device. Turnera can sync this data between your devices using your iCloud account. Data synced this way is stored in your own iCloud account and is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. The developer of Turnera cannot access it.

Apple diagnostics

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers on your Apple device, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports according to Apple’s own Privacy Policy. Turnera does not collect these reports itself.

Third-party services

Turnera does not integrate any third-party services that collect personal information.

Children’s privacy

Turnera is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Turnera evolves. Any future changes will be published on this page, with the date above updated.
